Traditional funeral insurance is built around premiums, underwriting, and profit. Our fund works differently: it is owned by its members, and every dollar collected for a funeral goes directly to that family. How the fund works

Three steps stand between a member's family and the support they need.

01

You join the fund

Sign up, add the payment method you'd like charged, and you're a member in good standing — no premiums, no medical underwriting.

02

A member passes away

The family notifies the fund and shares the actual cost of the janazah and burial. The fund divides that cost equally across every active member.

03

Everyone contributes their share

Each member's stored payment method is charged its equal portion automatically, and the full amount is sent to support the family — usually within a day.
